Self-hosted · Apache-2.0 · Bring any model

Your private Jarvis.

Ava is a personal AI operating layer: chat, voice, GPU workloads, and app automation behind one dashboard — on your hardware, with the model of your choice (local vLLM / Ollama / llama.cpp, or a cloud key).

Not a chatbot. Not a model. A control layer for your own AI.

Get started View on GitHub Why Ava?

  • Voice, gated to you

    Speech in, speech out — fully on-device (Whisper STT, Piper TTS). Enroll a voiceprint and a biometric gate makes Ava answer your voice, not anyone within earshot.

  • Drives your other apps

    Drop in a small connector manifest and Ava monitors and drives the app — health, metrics, agent tools, and its egress policy are all generated for you. No core-code changes.

  • MCP, but governed

    Point a manifest at any MCP server and its tools go live — behind an auto-generated egress policy. The sandboxed agent reaches exactly two policed routes. Other clients trust the server; Ava contains it.

  • Edits its own code, governed

    Ava writes source changes as git commits — every change one revert away. By default each one waits for your approval (code.approval); secrets and models are never writable.

  • A real Command Center

    Tokens/sec, time-to-first-token, render times, cost & energy, live jobs, alerts, approvals. An assistant you can't observe is one you can't trust.

  • Private by default

    Single-tenant on your GPU, bound to localhost, zero telemetry. Conversations, files, and your voiceprint stay on your box — nothing leaves unless you wire it out yourself.

See everything it does

The dashboard is the front door: a Vitals tab (her performance — throughput, latency, cost & energy, hardware) and an Operations tab (live jobs, connectors, alerts, and the approval-gated Control Center). Setup happens in the browser too — pick a model, provision the agent, wire in apps, enroll your voice.
