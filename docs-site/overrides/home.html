Meet Ava

Your AI. Your hardware.

Chat, voice, GPU workloads, and app automation in one private dashboard. Runs on your machine with any model: vLLM, Ollama, llama.cpp, or a cloud key.

A control layer for your own AI.

{#- deploy/README.md builds to deploy/ (MkDocs maps README.md to the directory index), so link the directory, not deploy/README/. -#} Get started View on GitHub Why Ava?

  • Voice, gated to you

    Speech in, speech out, fully on-device. Enroll a voiceprint and Ava answers your voice only.

  • Drives your other apps

    Drop in a small manifest. Ava monitors the app, charts its metrics, and calls its actions. No core changes.

  • MCP, but governed

    Wire in any MCP server and its tools go live behind an auto-generated egress policy. The agent reaches two policed routes, nothing else.

  • Edits its own code, governed

    Source changes land as git commits. Each waits for your approval by default; secrets and models are never writable.

  • A real Command Center

    Tokens/sec, latency, cost and energy, live jobs, alerts, approvals. If you can't observe it, you can't trust it.

  • Private by default

    Single-tenant, localhost-bound, zero telemetry. Nothing leaves your box unless you wire it out.
