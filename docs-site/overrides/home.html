One command to install. Everything after that is browser setup. Why Ava?

  • Voice, gated to you

    Speech in, speech out, fully on-device. Enroll a voiceprint and Ava answers your voice only.

    • {#- Four cards, not six. "MCP, but governed" folded into the card below (the acronym is kept so a developer still scans it, but it no longer spends a whole card on a term a novice cannot read), and "Private by default" folded into the hero tagline, where the promise belongs. -#}

  • Drives the apps you already run

    Point Ava at an app and it becomes a tab: Ava watches it, charts it, and calls its actions. Any MCP server plugs in the same way, fenced behind an auto-generated egress policy.

  • You can see everything it does

    Tokens/sec, latency, cost and energy, live jobs, alerts, approvals. If you can't observe it, you can't trust it.

  • Improves itself, with your approval

    Source changes land as git commits. Each waits for your approval by default; secrets and models are never writable.
