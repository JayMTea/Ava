Connect an app. Get its tools, its dashboard, and its firewall.

Anyone can build an app now, so you have five of them and nothing joins them up. Point Ava at one: a single manifest gives it a tab in your sidebar, a health row, a live performance chart, the tools your AI is allowed to call, and an allow-list naming every address those tools may reach. Ava writes every one of them for you, from that one file.

Free and open source. On your hardware, on your model, with the agent sandboxed.
